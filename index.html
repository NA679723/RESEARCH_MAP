    How this was made

    Papers were gathered on 25 October 2026 by eight parallel web-search passes (identifiability theory, temporal models, disentanglement and generative models, robustness and invariance, applications and LLMs, latent structure learning, plus two gap-filling passes for RL/interpretability and science/vision/graphs), drawing on arXiv, OpenReview, PMLR, ML Anthology, workshop pages and reading lists. After merging duplicate titles, 703 papers remained. Each paper's title (weighted twice) and a short paraphrased summary were turned into TF-IDF vectors with unigrams and bigrams, reduced to 100 dimensions with LSA, and grouped with k-means. k = 20 gave the best cosine silhouette between 8 and 20. Keywords come from class-based TF-IDF; topic names were written by reading each cluster's keywords and member titles. The map is a UMAP projection of the same vectors, lightly guided by the cluster labels so each topic reads as one region; distances between regions are approximate. Summaries are paraphrases, some written from citing papers rather than the original abstract, and the collection is broad but not exhaustive; some adjacent fields (treatment effects, IRM, deconfounded vision) are included because they apply causal ideas to representations.
