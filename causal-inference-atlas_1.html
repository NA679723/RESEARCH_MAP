How this map was made

Collection

Text features

Each paper is represented by its title (weighted double) plus the six most distinctive terms of its abstract, chosen by TF-IDF across the whole set, with two-word phrases such as "propensity score" kept intact.

Clustering and labels

Non-negative matrix factorization found 22 topics; one with fewer than 60 papers was folded into each paper's next-best topic. Labels and descriptions were written by reading each topic's top terms and its most central and most cited papers, then grouped into three families by similarity.

Map layout

Positions come from UMAP over three views of each paper: its topic mix, a latent-semantic projection of the terms, and word-vector meaning (spaCy's large English vectors). Nearby dots discuss similar things; distance between far-apart islands is only roughly meaningful.
