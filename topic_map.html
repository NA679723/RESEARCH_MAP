    How this was made

    This map is the earlier Fairness in ML topic map, converted to the same format as the other atlases. Paper titles, venues, years, first authors, topic assignments and map positions are kept exactly as in that map; its 26 topics are grouped into six themes (Bias in vision & multimodal models; Fair allocation & decision-making; Fair learning: definitions & methods; Ranking, graphs & federated systems; Robustness, privacy & subgroup performance; Social bias in language models), named at the end of each topic description. Topic keywords were computed here from the papers' titles with class-based TF-IDF, and the short topic names and descriptions were written by reading each topic's titles. The original map did not include links or summaries, so entries show only title, first author, year and venue.
