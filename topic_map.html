How this map was made

Collection

Selection

A paper is kept when its title names a fairness concept (fair or unfair, demographic parity, equalized odds, disparate impact, protected or sensitive attributes, social, gender or racial bias, stereotypes, envy-freeness, worst-group accuracy and similar), or its abstract uses those terms repeatedly. Uses like "a fair comparison" do not count.

Clustering and labels

Titles (weighted double) and abstracts became TF-IDF vectors over words and two-word phrases, reduced with LSA to 150 dimensions and UMAP to 10, then grouped by k-means into 26 topics, where the silhouette score levels off. Labels and descriptions were written by reading each topic's most distinctive terms and its most central papers, then grouped into six themes.

Map layout

Positions come from a separate two-dimensional UMAP of the same LSA vectors. Nearby dots use similar words; distance between far-apart islands is only roughly meaningful. Positions show word similarity, not citation links.
